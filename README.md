# Mortah (مرتاح)

**Ergonomic gaming chairs, office chairs, and desks, made in Egypt.**

---

## Project Name
Mortah (مرتاح)

## Project Idea
Mortah is a website and social media brand that sells locally made, health-supportive
office furniture in Egypt: gaming chairs, office chairs, and desks.

Most furniture in the Egyptian market is either overpriced when it is modern, or poor in
quality when it is affordable. Mortah brings modern, ergonomic designs made in Egypt at a
competitive price, with quality control, a one-year warranty, a 14-day return policy, and
delivery with installation.

**Our Why:** Comfort and trust are the right of everyone who spends their day at a desk.

**Target audience:** Gamers and content creators, freelancers, students, and companies.

**Launch market:** Cairo, Giza, Alexandria, and the northern governorates, then the rest of Egypt.

**What makes us different:**
- Ergonomic design for long hours of sitting
- Locally made with quality control (factory visits and product testing before launch)
- Prices about 20% below comparable branded alternatives
- Custom orders made to the customer's requirements
- A health-guidance booklet with every product
- Delivery assembled to the customer's door; payment by cash on delivery or InstaPay
